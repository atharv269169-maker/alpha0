📚 Notes & Study
01🧠Instant Notes Maker▼
Explain [topic] to me like I'm a 16 year old. Give me: - Key points in bullet form - 1 real life example - 3 most important things to remember for exam
Replace [topic] with any subject — works with every chapter!
02📄Chapter Summary in 2 Min▼
Summarize this chapter in under 200 words. Only include what's important for exam. Chapter: [paste chapter text here]
Paste entire chapters — AI filters only exam-worthy content!
03💡Concept Simplifier▼
I don't understand [concept]. Explain using a simple analogy or story. Then give me 3 practice questions on it.
Perfect for Physics, Chemistry, Math concepts!
04📐Formula Cheat Sheet▼
Create a cheat sheet of all important formulas and definitions for [subject] - [chapter]. Format it like a quick reference card.
Save the output — instant revision card!
05🃏Flashcard Creator▼
Create 10 flashcards for [topic]. Format: Front: [Question] | Back: [Answer] Make them exam-focused and concise.
Copy to Anki or Quizlet for spaced repetition!
06🗺️Mind Map Builder▼
Create a text mind map for [topic]. Show: Main topic → subtopics → key points. Use indentation to show hierarchy.
Great for visual learners and essay planning!
07📖Difficult Word Explainer▼
I'm reading about [topic] and don't understand: [word1, word2, word3] Explain each in one simple sentence with example.
Use for any subject with technical vocabulary!
08📊1-Page Revision Notes▼
Create 1-page revision notes for [chapter]. Include: key concepts, important dates/numbers, common exam questions, and memory tricks.
Print and stick on wall before exams!
09🔗Topic Connector▼
How is [topic A] related to [topic B]? Explain connection and why understanding both together helps in exams.
Builds deep understanding, not just memorization!
10🎯Exam Pattern Analyzer▼
These are last year's exam questions on [topic]: [paste questions] What patterns do you see? What should I focus most?
Use 3-5 years of PYQs for best results!
✍️ Assignments & Projects
11📋Assignment Outline Generator▼
I have to write an assignment on [topic] — [word count] words. Create outline: introduction, 3-4 main sections, conclusion. Subject: [subject name]
Start every assignment with this — saves hours!
12✏️Introduction Writer▼
Write a strong introduction for assignment on [topic]. Sound like a student — not too formal. Word count: 100 words.
Let AI break the blank page block!
13🔍Research Point Finder▼
I'm writing about [topic]. Give me 10 strong points I can use in my assignment with brief explanations for each.
Use these as your paragraph topics!
14🏁Conclusion Maker▼
Write a conclusion for assignment on [topic]. Summarize: [point 1, point 2, point 3] Keep it under 100 words.
Add your own twist to make it genuine!
15💡Project Idea Generator▼
Give me 10 unique project ideas for [subject] - [grade]. For each: what to make, materials, difficulty, and time required.
Pick the one that matches your skill level!
16🔄Plagiarism-Safe Rewriter▼
Rewrite this paragraph in my own words. Keep meaning same but change sentence structure. Paragraph: [paste text here]
Always add your own examples after rewriting!
17📑Case Study Analyzer▼
Analyze this case study for [subject]: [paste case study] Give: main problem, stakeholders, solutions, best recommendation.
Works for Business Studies, Economics, Law!
18👥Group Project Divider▼
Group project on [topic] with [X] members. Divide work fairly — give each person tasks with deadlines over [X] weeks.
Share output with your whole group!
19📚Bibliography Formatter▼
Format these sources in [APA/MLA] style for my bibliography: [paste your sources here]
Saves 30 mins of manual citation work!
20⭐Answer Improver▼
Make this answer better — more points, better language, proper structure. My answer: [paste] Question: [paste]
Use after writing your draft — not before!
🎯 Exam Preparation
21📝Mock Test Creator▼
Create 20-question mock test for [subject] - [chapter]. Include: 5 MCQs, 5 short, 5 long, 5 application. Give answer key at the end.
Best way to test yourself before the real exam!
22⚠️Weak Topic Identifier▼
I got these marks/made these mistakes: [details] What are my weak areas? What should I study more before exam?
Be honest — AI gives better advice that way!
23📅3-Day Revision Plan▼
Exam for [subject] in 3 days. Topics: [list topics]. Make hour-by-hour study plan for all 3 days.
Include breaks — AI will remind you to rest!
24🧩Tricky Question Predictor▼
Based on [syllabus/chapter list], what are top 10 tricky questions a teacher might ask? Give ideal answers for each.
Teachers repeat patterns — this helps you spot them!
25🧠Memory Trick Maker▼
Create funny mnemonics to remember: [list — dates, formulas, names, lists] Make them funny and easy to recall.
The sillier the mnemonic, the better you remember!
26👀Examiner's Perspective▼
What does examiner want when they ask: [question]? What key points MUST be in answer to get full marks? Subject: [subject]
Think like the examiner — not just the student!
27⏱️Exam Time Strategy▼
Exam is [X] hours with [X] questions worth [X] marks each. How should I divide my time? Give strategy.
Practice timed tests with this strategy!
28🚨Last Minute Cram▼
2 hours before [subject] exam. Topics: [list]. Give absolute minimum I must know to pass.
Emergency only — study properly first!
29🎤Self Quiz Mode▼
Ask me 10 questions on [topic] one by one. After I answer each, tell if I'm right and explain the correct answer.
Type your answer — don't just read the question!
30📈Score Improvement Plan▼
I scored [X]% in last [subject] test. Want to score [Y]% next time. What specific changes should I make?
Be specific about what went wrong last time!

Cover letter for internship at [company] for role of [role]. My skills: [X]. Why I want this: [X].
Research the company — mention something specific!

Studying [subject], interested in [interests]. What career paths are best for me? Top 5 with salary, skills needed, how to start.
Explore multiple paths before committing!
39🏫College Comparison▼
Compare colleges for [course]: [college 1, college 2, college 3] Criteria: placement, fees, reputation, location.
Visit campuses before final decision!

Build skills now — don't wait to finish college!

Daily schedule for student: College: [X hrs], Study: [X hrs], Hobbies: [X], Sleep: [X hrs]. Make it realistic and sustainable.
Try for 1 week then adjust!

Watch first, then organize — not the other way!
